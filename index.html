Camera-Controlled Video Generation

Supplementary videos for the anonymous ICLR 2027 submission “Camera Positional Embedding for Test-Time Training”.

Task. Given a source video of a scene, the model generates the same scene seen along a target camera trajectory (held-out pairs of MultiCamVideo). The target video on the right of the top row is the ground truth.

Models. All four models are the same autoregressive video model with LaCT TTT layers, trained with the same data and schedule (14k steps); they differ only in the camera embedding. Every method is sampled with the same seed per pair (40 Euler steps, no guidance).

No selection. All 64 held-out pairs are shown in their fixed order. RotErr is the per-pair rotation error (degrees) of the camera trajectory that COLMAP recovers from the generated video, when COLMAP registers it.
